BrainMary™ Ingredients

  1. Magnesium Stearate:  Magnesium Stearate is a commonly used excipient in dietary supplement manufacturing, primarily serving as a lubricant and flow agent during production. It helps prevent ingredients from sticking to manufacturing equipment and supports consistent processing and capsule production. In BrainMary, its role is primarily functional rather than nutritional, helping support reliable manufacturing and consistent formulation. Magnesium stearate is not included as a primary brain-health ingredient, and its presence should not be interpreted as providing direct cognitive benefits.
  2. Huperzia Serrata: Huperzia Serrata is a plant source of Huperzine A, a naturally occurring compound that has been studied for its effects on acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter involved in memory and learning. Huperzine A is known to inhibit acetylcholinesterase, the enzyme responsible for breaking down acetylcholine. In BrainMary, Huperzia Serrata is included to provide nutritional support for memory, learning, focus, and overall cognitive wellness. Research has investigated Huperzine A for its potential role in supporting cognitive function, although individual results may vary and research on isolated compounds should not be interpreted as proof of specific results from the complete BrainMary formula.
  3. Rice Flour: Rice Flour is a commonly used inactive ingredient, filler, or carrier in dietary supplement formulations. In BrainMary, it serves a functional role by helping provide consistency, stability, and proper distribution of the formula’s active ingredients in each serving. Unlike the botanical and nutritional ingredients, rice flour is not included as a primary cognitive-support ingredient, but rather to help maintain the overall quality and consistency of the finished supplement
  4.  Rhodiola Root: Rhodiola Root (often *Rhodiola rosea*) is an adaptogenic botanical traditionally used to support the body's response to everyday stress and promote overall resilience. In BrainMary, Rhodiola Root is included to provide nutritional support for mental stamina, cognitive wellness, and the body's natural antioxidant defenses. Its adaptogenic properties have been studied for their potential role in supporting focus, reducing occasional fatigue, and maintaining balanced cognitive performance during periods of everyday mental demand.
  5. Theobromine: Theobromine is a naturally occurring methylxanthine found in cacao and is known for its mild stimulant properties. In BrainMary, theobromine is included as part of the formula’s cognitive wellness support, providing nutritional support for alertness, attention, and mental focus. Unlike caffeine, theobromine has a different activity profile and is generally considered less stimulating, making it a useful ingredient to include in a balanced cognitive wellness formula. Individual responses can vary depending on sensitivity, diet, and other sources of stimulants.
  6. Bacopa Monnieri: Bacopa Monnieri is a well-known botanical used in Ayurvedic wellness traditions and widely studied for its potential role in memory, learning, focus, and cognitive wellness. In BrainMary, Bacopa Monnieri is included to provide nutritional support for memory and mental clarity while complementing a calm, balanced approach to everyday cognitive health. Research suggests that Bacopa’s potential cognitive effects may develop gradually with consistent use, making it a suitable ingredient for a daily brain wellness routine rather than an immediate stimulant-based approach.
  7. Green Coffee Bean : Green Coffee Bean is a notable botanical ingredient in BrainMary and a natural source of chlorogenic acids, plant compounds recognized for their antioxidant properties. Included in the formula, green coffee bean provides nutritional support for mental alertness and overall cognitive wellness while complementing the body's natural defenses against oxidative stress. Its inclusion adds botanical support to BrainMary’s broader approach to focus, mental clarity, and everyday brain health.
  8.  L-Tyrosine : L-Tyrosine is an amino acid that serves as a precursor to neurotransmitters such as dopamine and norepinephrine, which play important roles in attention, motivation, mood, and cognitive function. In BrainMary, L-Tyrosine is included to provide nutritional support for focus, mental clarity, and cognitive wellness, particularly during periods of everyday mental demand or stress. Its role in neurotransmitter production makes it a relevant component of a formula designed to support sustained mental performance and overall brain health.
